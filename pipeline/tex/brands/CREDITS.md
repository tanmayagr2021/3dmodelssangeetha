# Credits for the brand pack textures

Every texture in this folder is cut from a front-of-pack photo on Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), contributed by its volunteers and licensed CC-BY-SA (the Open Food Facts image licence). The photos were cropped, straightened in lighting and resized; no other change was made to the artwork.

The brand names, logos and packaging designs shown (Amul, Nandini, Aavin, Heritage, iD, Real, Maaza, Coca-Cola, Fanta, Sprite, Thums Up, Bisleri, Mother's Recipe, Sakthi, Viswas, Nitya, Fresho and others) are trademarks of their owners and appear only to illustrate an everyday Indian fridge in a retail demonstration. No endorsement is implied.

## Nandhana Palace and RNR Biryani (restaurant brands)
Logos and packaging art taken from the brands' own websites: https://nandhanarestaurants.com/ and https://rnrbiryani.com/. The names, logos and packaging designs are
trademarks of their owners. They are shown with the owner's permission for the client's demo: the user told us that the owner of
the client company owns both brands. Files: nandana_logo, nandana_box_front, nandana_tub_wrap (a re-created logo label, not a photograph),
rnr_logo, rnr_box_front, rnr_tin_lid (re-arranged official art). No food photographs are used: the fridge is vegetarian plus eggs only.
Official spelling is "Nandhana Palace"; the files use the spelling "nandana" as asked.
